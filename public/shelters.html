📋 What Else You Need to Do & Pay (Items NOT Included in Any Fee)

Every shelter handles spay/neuter and initial shots, but you are responsible for the following municipal bylaws, veterinary follow-ups, and setup expenses:

🏷️ 1. City of Calgary Pet Licence

Legally mandatory under Calgary's Responsible Pet Ownership Bylaw for all cats over 3 months old. (Included FREE for 6 months ONLY at City Animal Services).

Cost: $21 / year (spayed/neutered)

💉 2. Follow-Up Vaccine Boosters

Kittens under 16 weeks receive their 1st dose at the shelter, but require booster shots (FVRCP booster at 12 & 16 wks, Rabies at 16 wks) at a vet clinic.

Cost: $75 – $140 per booster visit

🚗 3. Approved Pet Carrier

All Calgary shelters strictly require a secure hard-shell or zipper carrier to take a cat out the door. Shelters sell cardboard boxes for $12–$15 if forgotten.

Cost: $30 – $50 (Buy once, reusable)

🥣 4. Core Kitten Starter Supplies

Litter box & scoop ($20), non-clumping kitten litter ($15), stainless steel food/water bowls ($15), scratching post/pad ($25), kitten food ($30).

Cost: $80 – $130 total setup

🩺 5. Free Post-Adoption Vet Exam

Money-Saving Tip: Many Calgary private vet clinics (such as VCA Canada clinics and local practices) offer a Complimentary Initial Health Exam within 10–14 days of shelter adoption! Bring your adoption contract.

Cost: $0 (Saves $85–$120 exam fee)

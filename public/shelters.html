💡 Why Adopting from a Calgary Rescue is an Incredible Value

While kitten adoption fees range between $169 and $260, each shelter invests over $800 to $1,200+ in professional veterinary care for every kitten before adoption:

✂️ Spay/Neuter: ~$400–$600 at private clinics
💉 Vaccinations: ~$150–$250 (FVRCP + Rabies)
🏷️ Microchip: ~$75–$100
🩺 Exam & Deworming: ~$120–$180

Every adoption fee directly pays forward the rescue of the next cat in Calgary!
